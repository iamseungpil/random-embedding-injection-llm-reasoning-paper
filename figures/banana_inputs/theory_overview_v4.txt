CAPTION TEXT (to be embedded as figure caption):
"Schematic mechanism of RSP. Layer-wise Hopfield-attention dynamics produce a deterministic trajectory (red) that is trapped in the basin of its initial state. RSP injects an isotropic random kick η at each layer, decomposed via the same scalar w_r^(l) that also controls its dilution. Because η is random in direction (Theorem 1: minimax coverage), it has positive probability of crossing any barrier; because w_r anneals as real-key context grows, kick magnitude decays automatically toward later layers. The result is simulated-annealing-like exploration that can reach deeper basins (green trajectory ending at m2) without an external schedule. We illustrate the qualitative mechanism; we do not claim a formal escape rate."
