\section{Conclusion}\label{sec:conclusion}

We analyzed Random Soft Prompts (RSPs), continuous vectors drawn from an isotropic Gaussian fitted to the entrywise statistics of the pretrained embedding table and injected without any training. The analysis proceeded through a lower envelope for early exploration (§\ref{sec:improve}), an upper envelope and annealing for late convergence (§\ref{sec:converge}), and the combination of isotropy, surrogate-level branch opening, and independent resampling that closes with the exponential saturation lower bound $\Pr(\mathrm{Pass@}N) \ge 1 - \exp(-N\,p_{\min})$ (§\ref{sec:multipath}). The three experiments of §\ref{sec:experimental_analysis} confirmed the predictions of this inequality: baseline accuracy recovery, the auto-annealing pattern, and the disappearance of accumulation when sharing a single RSP. Because RSP is rank-changing while temperature is rank-preserving, the two are complementary, suggesting a sampling-stage remedy for the entropy collapse in GRPO \citep{shao2024deepseekmath} beyond DAPO \citep{yu2025dapo} and CE-GPPO \citep{su2025cegppo}. Training-time validation against these baselines, extension beyond mathematical reasoning, and a principled choice of RSP length and injection position remain open.
